Family Organizer

A private home organizer for a household: one shared calendar, chores, lists, meals, and photos, shown on a wall tablet in the kitchen and on everyone's phones.

Open Family Organizer

Signing in with Google is needed to open your household. This page and the policies below are public.

What it does

  • Family calendar: everyone's events in one place, color-coded by person, as a day, week, or month view. Events can be added by hand, from calendar links (school, sports, holidays, Apple Calendar), or from Google Calendar.
  • Chores and rewards: daily and weekly chores per person, with stars that kids can spend on rewards.
  • Lists: a shared grocery list sorted by store aisle, and to-do lists.
  • Meals and recipes: a weekly meal plan, the family's recipes and cookbooks, a "what can I make?" search, and a cooking mode with kitchen timers.
  • Weather and countdowns: today's forecast with rain timing, the week ahead, and countdowns to trips and birthdays.
  • Photos: a family photo slideshow on the wall display when nobody is using it.
  • Reminders: optional email reminders before events and a daily summary.

How it uses your Google account

  • Sign-in: your Google name, email address, and profile photo identify you to the rest of your household.
  • Google Calendar (optional, read-only): if you choose "Connect my Google Calendar", the app reads your calendars' events (title, time, location) from 30 days ago to 120 days ahead and shows them on your household's shared calendar. It never creates, changes, or deletes anything in your Google Calendar, and you can disconnect at any time, which removes the copied events.
  • Your information is visible only to the people in your own household. It is never sold, shared, or used for ads.

Who it's for

Family Organizer is a free, non-commercial app run for a small number of invited households (friends and family of its administrator). Households join by invitation; it is not open for public sign-up.
