Privacy Policy

Family Organizer · Last updated October 3, 2026

Family Organizer is a private app that a small number of invited households (friends and family of the app's administrator) use to share a calendar, chores, meal plans, recipes, and photos on a wall display and on phones. It is not offered to the public, and each household's information is kept separate from every other household's. This page explains what the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics: name, email address, and profile photo, used to sign you in and show who is who.
  • Google Calendar events (read-only), only if you choose "Connect my Google Calendar": event titles, times, and locations from your primary calendar, from 30 days ago to 120 days ahead.
  • What your family enters: chores, lists, meal plans, recipes and cookbooks, photos, events added by hand, calendar links you add, and household settings such as time zone.

How it is used

Only to show this information to the members of your family inside the app, and to send reminder emails you turn on. Calendar access is read-only: the app never creates, changes, or deletes anything in your Google Calendar.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) in the United States. Security rules let only members of your own household read it, including the app's administrator, who cannot open another household's calendar, lists, photos, or anything else in it. To keep the app running and free, the administrator can see each household's name, the email of the person who started it, and how much it stores (numbers of events, recipes, and photos, and their total size), never their contents. The token that lets the app read your calendar is stored separately and can only be read by the app's server code, not by any person using the app.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data, show ads, or use it for anything other than running the app.

Google API Services

Family Organizer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Tap Disconnect on the Calendar tab to revoke calendar access and remove your synced events.
  • You can also revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • To have your account and everything you added deleted, contact the app's administrator using the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen.
